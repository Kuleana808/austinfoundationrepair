Foundation Repair FAQ

How much does foundation repair cost in Austin?

Foundation repair costs typically range from $3,500 to $15,000 depending on severity. Minor crack repairs may cost $500-$2,000, while extensive pier work can exceed $20,000. We provide free inspections and detailed quotes.

What are signs my foundation needs repair?

Common signs include cracks in walls or floors, doors/windows that stick, uneven floors, gaps between walls and ceiling, and exterior brick cracks. If you notice any of these, schedule a free inspection.

How long does foundation repair take?

Most repairs take 1-3 days. Minor crack repairs can be done in hours, while full pier installation may take up to a week for larger homes.

Is foundation repair covered by insurance?

Damage from sudden events like plumbing leaks may be covered, but gradual settling typically isn't. We can work with your insurance to document damage and submit claims.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, lifetime transferable warranties on all pier and beam repairs, covering workmanship and materials.

Can I sell my house with foundation problems?

Yes, but foundation issues reduce value and buyer interest significantly. Repairs before selling typically provide better ROI and faster sales.

What causes foundation problems in Austin?

Austin's expansive clay soil shrinks during dry periods and expands when wet, causing settling, cracking, and structural movement.

Should I get multiple quotes?

Yes, get 2-3 quotes to compare. Focus on licensed, insured contractors with good reviews and warranties. Beware of extremely low quotes.
